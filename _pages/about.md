I work as a Research Associate at the [Small Area Health Statistics Unit](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/school-public-health/epidemiology-and-biostatistics/small-area-health-statistics-unit/) in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at Imperial College London, working with [Dr Bethan Davies](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/bethan.davies06). My project is to evaluate the health impact of London's Ultra Low Emission Zone on children and young people with asthma. I am funded by the MRC Centre for Environment and Health and NIHR Health Protection Research Units in Environmental Exposures and Health. You can also find me [here](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/c.cai20).

I completed my PhD at the Department of Primary Care and Public Health at Imperial. I was under the supervision of [Professor Christopher Millett](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/c.millett) and [Dr Thomas Hone](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.hone). My PhD project evaluated the impacts of China's primary health care reforms since 2009 on the health system and health outcomes. During my PhD, I collaborated with researchers from the George Institute for Global Health in Australia and China, Peking University, and Harbin Medical University. I was engaged in a chronic disease management project in rural China, the [SAPPHIRE](https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-research/research-projects/sapphire-study-strengthening-chinas-rural-public-health-services-for-hypertension-and-diabetes-care) project, led by the Chinese Center for Disease Control and Prevention and the George Institute for Global Health.

I am also trained as a sociologist for seven years. Sociology has shaped who I am and what I care about in a substantial way. It is what got me interested in health inequities and makes me transit to public health. It is the lens through which I observe the world and myself.
