## PhD Project: China's Primary Care Reform

- **Aim**: Evaluating the health system and health impacts of China's primary care reform since 2009.
- **Publications**
    1. [Impact of china’s primary healthcare reforms on utilisation, payments and self-reported health: a quasi-experimental analysis of a middle-aged and older cohort 2011–2018.](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjph-2024-001595)
    2. [The association between china’s primary health care reform and inequalities in primary care utilisation and maternal mortality: a quasi-experimental longitudinal study from 2010 to 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12939-025-02541-z)
    3. [Health and health system impacts of China’s comprehensive primary healthcare reforms: a systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1093/heapol/czad058)
